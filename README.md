# Pakistan Heritage AI Platform

### AI-Powered Guide to Pakistan's History, Heritage, and Cultural Legacy

The **Pakistan Heritage AI Platform** is an AI-powered chatbot designed to make Pakistan's history, cultural heritage, archaeological sites, traditions, and historical civilizations more accessible and engaging.

The platform provides users with an interactive way to explore Pakistan's rich cultural and historical legacy through conversational AI.

## Key Features

- AI-powered conversational guide
- Information about Pakistan's historical heritage
- Exploration of archaeological and UNESCO heritage sites
- Historical civilizations and periods
- Cultural traditions and legacy
- Interactive question-and-answer experience
- Immersive conversational exploration of Pakistan's heritage
- Web-based chatbot interface

## Technology

- **Botpress** – AI chatbot development and conversational interface
- **Conversational AI** – Interactive heritage exploration
- **Web-based Interface** – Accessible through a browser

## Example

The chatbot can provide information about historical locations such as **Taxila**, including:

- Location and heritage status
- UNESCO World Heritage significance
- Historical civilizations
- Major historical periods
- Archaeological sites
- Cultural and religious significance

## Purpose

The project aims to use artificial intelligence to make Pakistan's history and cultural heritage more accessible through an interactive and engaging digital experience.

It combines **technology, AI, education, and cultural heritage** to encourage people to learn about and explore Pakistan's historical legacy.
